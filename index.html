About

I am currently a PhD student at the Shanghai Innovation Institute (SII), jointly trained with Southeast University (SEU). I am primarily advised by Prof. Lixin Yang and Prof. Cewu Lu, with Prof. Lifeng Zhu as my joint-training advisor. I am also working closely with Yutao Sun (THU) on unified multimodal architectures and autoregressive video generation. I got my B.Eng at the School of Instrument Science and Engineering, Southeast University in 2023. My research interests include Embodied AI & Robot Learning, Multimodal Understanding & Generation. I'm always looking for related collaboration. If you are interested in chatting with me, feel free to drop me an email.
